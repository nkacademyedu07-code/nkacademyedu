Know Exactly Where You Lost Marks

NK Academy's Paper Review goes beyond a score — it shows you precisely why marks were lost and what to do next.

Paper Review — Class 10 Maths
Q4(b): Find the area of a triangle with vertices A(1,2), B(4,6), C(7,2). [3 marks]
Area = ½|x₁(y₂−y₃)+x₂(y₃−y₁)+x₃(y₁−y₂)|
= ½|1(6−2)+4(2−2)+7(2−6)|
= ½|4+0+−28|
= ½×−24 = −12
❌
CONCEPTUAL ERROR
Sign convention confused — area cannot be negative. Absolute value must be applied before multiplying by ½.
⚠️
WEAK AREA IDENTIFIED
Coordinate Geometry — formula application needs focused revision.
✅
RECOMMENDED ACTION
Study 5 worked examples on coordinate area. Practice 10 problems. Review in next assessment.
